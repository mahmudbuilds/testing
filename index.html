ZERO WASTE LOGISTICS

How Our Courier Works

01

Harvested at Dawn

Greens, vegetables, and heritage grains are harvested every morning before 7 AM from our 12 regional growers. Nothing spends days in a commercial cold-chain warehouse.

02

Cooked to Order at 4 PM

Our culinary team braises, roasts, and bakes each supper in French copper and seasoned cast iron. Meals are ladled directly into vacuum-insulated stoneware canisters.

03

Electric Trike Hand-off

Delivered within 35 minutes by our dedicated courier fleet. Your meal arrives piping hot. When you order next, simply hand back the empty containers for sanitization.

NATURAL FERMENTATION

Bread Baked for Digestion and Flavor

Commercial bread relies on fast synthetic yeast. Our loaves ferment for 36 hours using a wild levain culture started in 2018. The result is an open, custard-like crumb and caramelized blistered crust that is gentle on your gut.
