ZERO-PLASTIC REVOLUTION

The 3-Tier Insulated Brass Tiffin Canister

Disposable plastic containers destroy the soul of hot West African food — making plantains soggy and letting precious palm oil cool into film. We deliver every supper in heavy triple-latched stainless steel tiffins with brass handles.

Tier 01

Base: Steaming Firewood Rice & Swallows

Maintains high thermal mass at the bottom, keeping jollof grains separate, dry, and fragrant.

Tier 02

Core: Deep Stoneground Egusi & Efo Riro

Airtight silicone seal prevents cross-spill and retains hot steam at over 80°C.

Tier 03

Crown: Charcoal Suya & Caramelized Dodo

Separated from moisture so the kuli-kuli bark stays crisp and plantains stay tender.
