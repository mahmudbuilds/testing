PIPING HOT LOGISTICS

The 30-Minute Courier Method

01

Ground Fresh at Dawn

Melon seeds, Scotch bonnets, and kuli-kuli peanut cakes are stoneground at 6 AM. We don't use powdered industrial seasoning packets or frozen bases.

02

Cast-Iron Firewood Simmer

Jollof rice and egusi are cooked in heavy cast iron to produce the authentic smoky party aroma that defines traditional Nigerian open-fire cooking.

03

Double-Walled Tiffins

Ladled directly into vacuum-insulated stainless steel canisters. Handed to your courier in electric trikes and delivered boiling hot within 30 minutes.
